## Results

- No sentence exceeds 22 words.
- No copy contains: leverage, seamless, effortless, robust, powerful, intuitive, reimagine, supercharge, delightful, journey, ecosystem, or “AI-powered”.
- Headings name the task or section. Buttons name their action or result.
- The first screen states the job, audience, first action, next result, privacy, offline behavior, and price in one view.
