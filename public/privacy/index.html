Privacy, in plain language

Project names, outcomes, evidence links, timer state, and receipt details stay in this browser.

What leaves your device

The app sends no work-session data, analytics, or tracking requests. It does not fetch evidence links. A link opens only when you select it.

Offline storage

The browser stores work sessions, receipt details, and the app files needed offline. Clearing site data removes them. Export a backup before clearing the browser or moving devices.

PDFs and exports

PDF, JSON, and CSV files are created on your device. Your browser downloads them.

Demo data

Demo work sessions use a separate browser database. Reset demo restores that sample. Start for real discards the demo database and opens your records.

Your choices

You can edit or delete a work session, export your data, or clear the site data through your browser.
