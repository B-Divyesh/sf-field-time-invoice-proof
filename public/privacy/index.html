Privacy, in plain language

Work Receipt is built to keep your work record under your control. Your sessions, project names, outcomes, evidence links, timer state, receipt identity, and license token are stored locally in your browser. We do not operate an account or synchronization service for this app.

What leaves your device

Nothing in your work log is sent to us. When you choose to verify or buy a Studio license, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API with the license token or opens its hosted checkout. Your work records are never included. The merchant of record may process purchase information under its own policy.

Offline storage

The app uses IndexedDB, local storage, a cache, and a service worker so it can run offline. Clearing site data removes these local records. Use Export backup before clearing the browser or moving devices.

Evidence links and PDFs

Evidence links are stored as text and are not fetched by Work Receipt. A link opens only when you select it. PDFs and exports are generated on your device and downloaded through your browser.

Analytics and retention

There are no tracking cookies, behavioral analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party fonts/scripts. Because we do not receive your work data, we cannot recover or delete it for you.

Your choices

You can edit or delete individual sessions, export JSON or CSV at any time, remove a stored license, or clear all site data through your browser. Questions can be raised through the repository linked by the product publisher.
